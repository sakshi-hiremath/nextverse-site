Sales Management Product

Field CRM

Field CRM is a sales-focused product designed to streamline the sales process, particularly for teams working in the field.

Core Capabilities

Lead Capture

Records leads entered by salespeople directly on-site in real time with quick contact logging.

Instant Quotations

Helps generate accurate, professional sales quotations quickly in seconds during field visits.

Follow-up Management

Supports the structured organization and timely execution of sales follow-ups with reminders.

Connected Workflow

Brings lead capture, quotation generation, and follow-up activities into one streamlined process.

Vision: Our vision for Field CRM is to simplify sales operations and help businesses manage their sales activities more efficiently.

Developed as an independent SaaS product rather than categorized as custom software or services delivered for individual clients.
